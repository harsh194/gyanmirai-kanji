GyanMirai Kanji App — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This policy covers the GyanMirai Kanji mobile app for Android and iOS (the "App"), published by GyanMirai ("we," "our," or "us"). It explains what data the App uses, who else receives it, and the choices you have. For the gyanmirai.com website, see our website Privacy Policy.

1. The short version

  • The App works fully without an account. Signing in with Google is optional.
  • If you don't sign in, your lessons, reviews and settings are stored only on your device and we do not receive them.
  • If you sign in, we store your account (email address and basic Google profile details) and a copy of your learning progress and study settings on our servers, so they can be backed up and synced across your devices.
  • You can delete your account and its synced data at any time in the App (You → Delete account) or by email.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID.
  • The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

Your study progress (which lessons you finished, review schedules and answers), your study settings, and the kanji and vocabulary content are kept in local databases on your device. If you don't sign in, this data is never uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

3. Your account (optional)

You can sign in with your Google account to back up your progress and sync it between devices. No feature of the App requires an account.

What we receive and store

  • Account details: when you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the App. Our backend stores your account as a user record containing your email address, the sign-in provider (Google), a Google account identifier, the basic profile details Google provides, and a random user ID we assign.
  • Learning progress: the review schedule for each kanji and word you have studied, your review history (which item, your answer grade and when), the lessons you have finished and your study settings. This is linked to your user ID.

How we use it

Only to provide the account: to sign you in, back up your progress and keep it in sync across your devices, and to let your "Remove ads" purchase follow your account. We do not use account or progress data for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Account and progress data are stored with Supabase, our backend provider, in its Tokyo (Japan) region, and are only accessible to your account. If you live outside Japan, this means your data is transferred to and processed in Japan. Supabase processes it on our behalf under its privacy policy.

Signing out and deleting your account

Signing out stops syncing; the progress on your phone stays. To delete your account, open You → Delete account in the App. This immediately deletes your account and all synced progress and settings from our servers. The progress on your phone is kept until you uninstall the App. You can also ask us to delete it by email; see how to delete your account.

4. Pronunciation audio

Audio clips are downloaded from audio.gyanmirai.com, served by Cloudflare. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address and basic device information to Cloudflare so the file can be delivered. The request does not contain any identifier linking it to you, and we do not use it to track you.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not bought "Remove ads", the App shows banner ads on the Home and Browse screens and a full-screen ad after you finish a lesson. Ads are never shown inside a lesson or review. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, where permitted)
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and operating system
  • ad impressions, clicks and diagnostic data

Google uses this data to show, measure and (with your consent) personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from You → Ad privacy choices in the App.
  • iOS: the App asks for permission before accessing your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Buying "Remove ads" stops all ad requests from the App.

6. Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never receive your card or bank details.

To confirm and restore your purchase, the App uses RevenueCat, which receives the store purchase receipt, an app user ID and basic device information (model, OS version, app version, country). If you are not signed in, the app user ID is an anonymous ID generated on your device; if you are signed in, it is your account's user ID, so your purchase follows your account. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The App is intended for teenagers and adults (13+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Security and retention

Data sent by the App travels over encrypted HTTPS connections, and synced data can only be read by the account it belongs to. On-device data stays until you uninstall the App.

If you sign in, your account and synced progress are kept until you delete your account. When you delete it, it is removed from our servers straight away, and from any backups within 30 days. Data held by Google, RevenueCat and Cloudflare is kept according to their policies linked above.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. If you have not signed in, we do not hold your study data on our servers, and most requests can be handled by the ad and purchase choices above or by uninstalling the App. If you have an account, you can delete it and its synced data at any time: use You → Delete account in the App or follow the steps on our account deletion page. For access, correction or anything else, email us from your account's email address and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Changes

If we change how the App handles your data, we will update this policy before the new version is released and change the effective date above.
