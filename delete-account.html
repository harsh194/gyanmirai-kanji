GyanMirai Kanji App — Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the GyanMirai Kanji mobile app (Android and iOS), published by GyanMirai. Signing in is optional; if you never signed in to the App, we hold no account or progress data from it and there is nothing to delete here. See also the GyanMirai Kanji App Privacy Policy.

1. Delete your account in the App

  1. Open the GyanMirai Kanji app and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all of its synced data are deleted from our servers immediately, and you are signed out on that device. Which account that is depends on your version of the App:

  • From the update that introduced GyanMirai accounts, the App signs you in with your GyanMirai account, the same account you use on gyanmirai.com and in our other apps that use GyanMirai accounts. Deleting it deletes it everywhere: you can no longer sign in with it on gyanmirai.com or in any GyanMirai app.
  • Earlier versions used a separate account for GyanMirai Kanji only. Deleting it there removes that account and its synced progress, favourites and settings; a gyanmirai.com account, if you have one, is not affected.

If GyanMirai Pro is still running

The App can't delete a GyanMirai account while GyanMirai Pro bought on gyanmirai.com is still running on it: a subscription (also after you cancel it, until the paid period ends) or an exam-season pass. The App's "Remove ads" purchase doesn't count. Cancel the subscription on gyanmirai.com (sign in, open Pricing and choose Manage subscription) and try again once your Pro has ended, or email hello@gyanmirai.com and we will delete the account for you.

2. Request deletion without the App

If you no longer have the App installed, send an email to hello@gyanmirai.comfrom the email address you signed in with (for Google sign-in, your Google account's address), with the subject "Delete my GyanMirai Kanji account". We will never ask for your password.

We delete the GyanMirai account with that address (on gyanmirai.com and in every GyanMirai app), and any account you created with a version of the App from before GyanMirai accounts were added, within 30 days of your request (usually much sooner) and reply to confirm. If the request comes from a different address, we may ask you to confirm it from the account's address first.

3. What is deleted

Deleting your GyanMirai account deletes:

  • Your account: email address, Google profile details (name, profile picture link) and user ID.
  • Everything this App synced: review schedules for each kanji and word, review history, finished lessons, favourites and study settings.
  • Your gyanmirai.com profile and what is saved to it on the website: study progress and practice and mock test results.
  • Whatever our other apps (GyanMirai Kana, GyanMirai JLPT Test) synced to the account.

In earlier versions, deleting the separate GyanMirai Kanji account deletes that account (email address, Google profile details and user ID) and its synced learning progress, favourites and study settings.

Deletion removes this data from our servers straight away, and from any backups within 30 days.

4. What is kept

  • On our servers: no account or progress data is kept after deletion. If you bought GyanMirai Pro on gyanmirai.com, the records of those payments are kept, by us and by our payment provider Lemon Squeezy.
  • An account from an earlier version: an account created by a version of the App from before GyanMirai accounts were added is separate and stays on our earlier backend. Deleting your GyanMirai account in the updated App does not remove it: delete it in that version of the App, or email us (see section 2) and we will delete it.
  • Purchases: the "Remove ads" purchase record stays with Google Play (or the App Store) and our purchase provider RevenueCat, so the purchase can still be restored through your store account. It contains no learning data.
  • On your phone: the progress stored locally in the App stays on the device until you uninstall the App or clear its data.
