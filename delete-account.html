GyanMirai Kanji App — Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the GyanMirai Kanji mobile app (Android and iOS), published by GyanMirai. Signing in is optional; if you never signed in, we hold no account or progress data for you and there is nothing to delete. See also the GyanMirai Kanji App Privacy Policy.

1. Delete your account in the App

  1. Open the GyanMirai Kanji app and sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all of its synced data are deleted from our servers immediately, and you are signed out on that device.

2. Request deletion without the App

If you no longer have the App installed, send an email to hello@gyanmirai.comfrom the email address of the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my GyanMirai Kanji account". We will never ask for your password.

We delete the account within 30 days of your request (usually much sooner) and reply to confirm. If the request comes from a different address, we may ask you to confirm it from the account's address first.

3. What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, Google profile details (name, profile picture link) and user ID.
  • All synced learning progress: review schedules for each kanji and word, review history and finished lessons.
  • Your synced study settings.

Deletion removes this data from our servers straight away, and from any backups within 30 days.

4. What is kept

  • On our servers: nothing. No account or progress data is kept after deletion.
  • Purchases: the "Remove ads" purchase record stays with Google Play (or the App Store) and our purchase provider RevenueCat, so the purchase can still be restored through your store account. It contains no learning data.
  • On your phone: the progress stored locally in the App stays on the device until you uninstall the App or clear its data.
